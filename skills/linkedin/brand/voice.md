# AI Ecoverse — Brand Voice

## Identity

AI Ecoverse is an open-source AI ecosystem project. We build tools that make AI agents practical and composable.

## Tone

- **Technical but human** — We know our stuff but don't hide behind jargon
- **Builder's voice** — We speak as people who ship, not people who theorize
- **Quietly confident** — Show the work, don't oversell it
- **Community-oriented** — "We" not "I", invite collaboration

## Register

- Professional but not corporate
- Direct, clear sentences
- Occasional dry wit is fine; never sarcastic or dismissive
- No exclamation marks unless genuinely excited (max 1 per post)

## Vocabulary

**Prefer:** build, ship, compose, orchestrate, automate, open-source, practical, real-world
**Avoid:** revolutionary, disrupting, game-changing, leverage (as verb), synergy, thought leader, delighted to announce

## Formatting

- 1-3 short paragraphs per post (not walls of text)
- Line breaks between ideas (LinkedIn renders these well)
- Hashtags: 2-4 max, at the end, always include #AIEcoverse
- Emoji: sparingly, functional not decorative (✅ for lists, → for flow)
- Links: one per post, inline with context

## What we don't do

- Humble-brag
- Engagement bait ("Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Like if you...")
- Reposts without adding original commentary
- Hot takes on trending controversies
- Selling or pitching directly
