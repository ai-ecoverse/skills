# AI Ecoverse — Content Pillars

## 1. Building in the open (40%)

What we're building, how it works, and why we made the choices we did.
Show the internals — architecture decisions, tradeoffs, real code.

**Angle:** "Here's how we solved X" not "Look what we built"
**Format:** Technical explanations with concrete examples

## 2. AI agent ecosystem (30%)

The broader landscape of AI agents, orchestration, and tooling.
Our perspective on where things are going and what matters.

**Angle:** Practitioner's view — what actually works vs. what's hype
**Format:** Observations, comparisons, lessons learned

## 3. Community & open source (20%)

Contributors, collaborations, and the open-source ethos.
Celebrating the ecosystem and inviting participation.

**Angle:** Gratitude and invitation, never gatekeeping
**Format:** Shoutouts, release notes, contribution guides

## 4. Behind the scenes (10%)

How we work, what our days look like, and the human side of building AI tools.

**Angle:** Authentic, not performative
**Format:** Short, specific vignettes

## What we never post about

- AI doom/safety debates (unless directly relevant to our work)
- Competitor bashing
- Hiring posts (unless genuinely relevant)
- Generic motivation/productivity advice
- Anything requiring a disclaimer about personal opinions
